Privacy Policy

Sudoku · Kracxify · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

Kracxify ("we", "us", "our") built the Sudoku app as a free-to-play mobile game supported by advertising and optional in-app purchases. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, the following third-party services used in the app may collect data automatically:

  • Google AdMob – serves advertisements and may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to deliver and measure ads.
  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases. Payment details are handled entirely by Google and are never seen or stored by us.

Game progress (puzzle state, statistics, streak) is saved locally on your device only using device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

How We Use Information

  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To process and verify in-app purchases via Google Play.
  • To save your game progress locally so you can continue where you left off.

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties beyond the services listed above.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may use your advertising ID and other data to show personalised ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Or visit: google.com/settings/ads

For more information see Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

The app offers optional purchases ("Remove Ads", hint packs) processed securely through the Google Play Store. We do not collect or store payment information. All transactions are governed by Google Play's Terms of Service.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention & Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app. We do not retain any data on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
